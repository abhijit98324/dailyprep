README.md

# DailyPrep — Govt Exam Daily Quiz & Mock Test Platform



DailyPrep is a mobile-first web application and installable PWA for Indian government job exam aspirants (**SSC, Banking, Railways, UPSC & State PSCs, Defence, Teaching, and State Police**).



All questions, explanations, quizzes, mock tests, and daily current affairs digests are **100% manually curated by the admin**—there is zero AI, LLM, or automated question generation.
